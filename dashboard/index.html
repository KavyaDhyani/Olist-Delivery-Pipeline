Decision Supported

The dashboard supports operational investigation of delivery performance by showing the overall late-delivery rate and separating fulfillment into purchase-to-carrier and carrier-to-customer stages. It does not establish the causal reason for an individual delay.

Methodology: KPI population: 96,281 delivered orders with complete required timestamps and a chronologically valid fulfillment sequence.
